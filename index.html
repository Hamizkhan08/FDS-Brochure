About Us
Built on Precision

Falcon Design Solutions is a dynamic team of steel detailers delivering comprehensive shop and field drawings for structural steel fabrication. Founded in 2019, we have rapidly grown into a globally trusted partner for fabricators, architects, and general contractors.

We specialize in structural steel, miscellaneous metals, BIM services, and connection design — leveraging cutting-edge 3D detailing software and AISC/NISD-approved practices to deliver results of uncompromising accuracy.

Our mission: precise, reliable shop drawings at a fair and competitive price — on time, every time.

Our Services
Precision Detailing for Smarter Structures
Structural Steel Detailing
Complete shop and erection drawings for beams, columns, braces, and primary structural members. 3D modelling, BOM, CNC/KISS/DXF file delivery included.
Miscellaneous Metals
Stairs, handrails, ladders, platforms, grating, bollards, ornamental metalwork, dumpster gates — precise detailing for all secondary steel.
Connection Design & Stamping
Engineer-reviewed bolted and welded connection designs for beams, columns, braces, trusses, and girders in full code conformance.
Estimation Support
Rapid, accurate material take-offs and estimation support to empower your bidding process with reliable quantity data.
BIM Services
Advanced Building Information Modeling services integrating steel detailing with multi-discipline coordination for clash-free construction.
